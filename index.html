Expansion & Factorization

  • Removing brackets: \(a(x+y) = ax+ay\)
  • Cross expansion: \((x+a)(x+b) = x^2+(a+b)x+ab\)

  • Diff. of 2 squares: \(a^2-b^2 = (a+b)(a-b)\)
  • Perfect square: \(a^2+2ab+b^2 = (a+b)^2\)
